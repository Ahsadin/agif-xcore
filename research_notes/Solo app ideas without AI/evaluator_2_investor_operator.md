# Evaluator 2 — "The Investor-Operator": independent evaluation of the 15 finalists and the BASELINE

Date: 2026-10-12. Evaluator background (hypothetical): former venture investor and ex-COO of a scaled small-business software company. I scored every criterion for every idea myself, using the coordinator's RULEBOOK unchanged. I did not open any `evaluator_*.md` file.

**How to read the evidence labels**
- **[E]**: confirmed from a search-engine extract attributed to the cited URL. I did not open the page. My two direct page fetches (alternativeto.net, capterra.at) failed with DNS errors and were not retried.
- **[L1]–[L5]**: taken from that lens file and not re-verified by me.
- **JUDGEMENT**: my own expertise, not verified this session.
- **Search budget:** all 15 web searches used. They went on competitor and substitute checks in German and Romanian, plus the baseline. No budget was left for heat pumps, SBV software, parent-run daycare apps, battery passports or tenant tools, so those notes rely on the lens files plus JUDGEMENT.

**Standard unit-economics conventions (applied to every idea)**
- **F (fixed business costs/month)**: a base of €1,500–2,000 (accountant, insurance, tools and hosting, legal reserve, marketing, travel).
  - Add €500–1,200 for a native-speaker freelancer wherever the buyer must be sold or supported in German, Romanian or Swiss German. The founder works in English.
  - Add liability insurance where errors could cost customers money.
- **Prices:** quoted net (B2B reverse charge or VAT-reclaiming buyers). Where the buyer cannot reclaim VAT (consumers, small teachers, non-profit associations), I treat the list price as gross and remove 19% German VAT.
- **CAC:** cash plus paid freelancer time. The founder's own time is excluded.
- **Formulas:**
  - Contribution = net ARPU × gross margin.
  - Customers needed N = (€8,000 + F) ÷ contribution.
  - LTV = contribution ÷ monthly churn.
- **Time to goal:** assumes the MVP launches around January 2027 (12-week build from today), with acquisition ramping. The probability of reaching €8k/month profit within 24 months is my JUDGEMENT.

> **Bottom line**
> - **Only two ideas reach TEST:** **L4-F1 care-home financing case manager (65.0)** and **L2-F1 CRA Logbook (62.0)**.
> - **Everything else is KILL by score**, including **BASE at 46.0**. No idea violates a hard rule on the evidence I could gather. Nine carry "borderline" H4/H8 watch-flags (see the summary table).
> - **14 of 15 finalists beat BASE on score**, but only 7 by 8 points or more. **L5-F3 (heat-pump check) scores below BASE.**
> - **Borderline KILLs:** **L1-A vet plans (59.0)** and **L5-F1 RambursFlip (58.0)** sit within one and two points of TEST. Each is cheap to test.
> - **€10,000 bet:** L4-F1. **Never fund:** L5-F3.
> - **Known issues:**
>   - **(a)** Lens 3 is directionally right about stable billing. German-capable tools with billing exist: Stabley (free) and EquineM (from €6). But H8 is not met, so it is a scoring kill, not a hard-rule kill.
>   - **(b)** The WEG logic partly carries over to Switzerland. Direct competitors exist and there is no neutral channel, but no funded or profitable player is shown. KILL by score.
>   - **(c)** Of the 12 German launches:

>     - 3 rest on weak or now-contradicted gap evidence.
>     - 3 are anchored (L2-F1, L2-F2, L5-F2). L2-F1 should launch EU-wide and English-first.

### L1-A — Monthly preventive-care plans for independent vet practices (Germany) — 59.0, KILL (one point from TEST)

**Hard rules: none violated.**
- **H1 pass:** deterministic plan rules, SEPA and reminders.
- **H2 pass:** a 12-week MVP is realistic.
- **H3 pass, conditional.**
  - A fixed-fee plan for scheduled preventive services is a service subscription, not insurance, as long as it carries no risk transfer (JUDGEMENT).

  - Lens 1's kill test 1 (a lawyer's written opinion) is the right gate.
- **H4 pass:** big tech is irrelevant. Practice-management vendors (easyVET, Vetera) could add plan modules. Neither lens 1 nor I checked this (gap).

  - My German search for monthly practice health plans found no German practice-plan provider.
